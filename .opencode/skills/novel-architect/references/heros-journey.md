# The Hero's Journey

The 12-step mythic structure for epic adventures.

## The Steps:

1. **Ordinary World:** Protagonist's normal life.
2. **Call to Adventure:** A challenge or opportunity is presented.
3. **Refusal of the Call:** Initial hesitation.
4. **Meeting the Mentor:** Guidance is received.
5. **Crossing the Threshold:** Leaving the ordinary world.
6. **Tests, Allies, and Enemies:** Navigating the "Special World."
7. **Approach to the Inmost Cave:** Pre-climax tension.
8. **The Ordeal:** The protagonist's greatest fear/central struggle.
9. **The Reward:** Seizing the "sword" or "elixir."
10. **The Road Back:** Returning with the prize.
11. **Resurrection:** The final test of the hero's new self.
12. **Return with the Elixir:** Returning changed and bearing a gift.
