# Beat Sheets (Save the Cat!)

Based on Blake Snyder's framework for high-impact pacing.

## The 15 Beats:

1. **Opening Image** (0-1%): Visual hook.
2. **Theme Stated** (5%): Hint at the novel's core message.
3. **Set-up** (1-10%): Introduce the "before" world.
4. **Catalyst** (10%): The life-changing incident.
5. **Debate** (10-20%): Protagonist questions their call to action.
6. **Break into Two** (20%): Committing to the journey.
7. **B Story** (22%): Usually a relationship or sub-plot.
8. **Fun and Games** (20-50%): Delivering the "promise of the premise."
9. **Midpoint** (50%): The stakes are raised; everything changes.
10. **Bad Guys Close In** (50-75%): External/internal pressure mounts.
11. **All Is Lost** (75%): The protagonist's lowest point.
12. **Dark Night of the Soul** (75-80%): Protagonist wallows but finds truth.
13. **Break into Three** (80%): A new plan emerges.
14. **Finale** (80-99%): The ultimate climax.
15. **Final Image** (100%): Contrast with the Opening Image.
