# Character Forge

A structure for building deep and consistent characters.

## The Profile Template:

1. **Name and Epithet:** (e.g., "Arthur, the Reluctant King")
2. **Core Desire:** What do they want more than anything?
3. **Fatal Flaw:** What is stopping them from getting it?
4. **The Lie:** What false belief do they have about themselves or the world?
5. **Inciting Secret:** Something they hope no one ever finds out.
6. **Speech Patterns:** Dialect, vocabulary level, common metaphors.
7. **The Arc:** How do they change from the beginning to the end?
