# The Snowflake Method

The Snowflake Method starts small and expands outward in logical steps.

## Workflow Steps:

1. **Step 1: One Sentence Summary** (1 hour)
   - Write a one-sentence summary of your novel. 
   - *Example: "A young boy discovers he is a wizard and must defeat a dark lord."*

2. **Step 2: One Paragraph Summary** (1 day)
   - Expand the sentence into a full paragraph describing the setup, the major disasters, and the ending.
   - Use the "Three Disasters and a Ending" structure.

3. **Step 3: Character Summaries** (1 day per character)
   - Create a one-page summary for each major character.
   - Include: Name, Motivation, Goal, Conflict, Epiphany.

4. **Step 4: One Page Synopsis** (Several days)
   - Expand each sentence of your Step 2 paragraph into its own paragraph.

5. **Step 5: Character Narratives** (1-2 days per character)
   - Write a one-page description of the story from each character's point of view.

6. **Step 6: Four Page Synopsis** (1 week)
   - Expand your one-page synopsis into a four-page summary.

7. **Step 7: Character Charts** (1 week)
   - Detailed charts for every character, including physical traits and history.

8. **Step 8: Scene List** (1-2 weeks)
   - Use a spreadsheet or document to list every scene you need to write.

9. **Step 9: Narrative Description** (Optional)
   - A multi-page narrative of each scene.

10. **Step 10: The First Draft**
    - Start writing!
